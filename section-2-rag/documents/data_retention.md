# Privacy and Data Retention

## Customer chat data

Customer support conversations are stored for 30 days by default.
After 30 days, the conversation content is automatically deleted from
the active support system.

Enterprise customers may configure a shorter retention period.

## Uploaded documents

Documents uploaded to the knowledge base remain stored until an
authorized administrator deletes them.

When a document is deleted, its associated vector embeddings are also
removed from the active vector store.

## Account deletion

A customer may request account deletion through the privacy settings
page. The request is normally completed within 30 days, subject to legal
or billing-related retention requirements.
