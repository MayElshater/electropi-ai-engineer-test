# Account Access and Support

## Password reset

A customer who cannot access their account should select "Forgot
Password" on the sign-in page.

The system sends a password-reset link to the email address associated
with the account. The link expires after 30 minutes.

If the email does not arrive, the customer should check the spam folder
and confirm that the correct email address was entered.

## Locked accounts

An account is temporarily locked after five unsuccessful login attempts.
The customer may try again after 15 minutes or contact support for
identity verification.

Support agents must never ask customers to send their password.

## Contacting support

Customers can contact support by email or through the in-app help form.
Standard support hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM UTC.
