# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Refund eligibility

Customers may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of the
initial subscription purchase.

A refund is available only when the account has not used more than
20 generated support answers during the refund period.

Renewal payments are normally non-refundable. However, customers may
contact support within 48 hours of an accidental renewal, and the
request will be reviewed individually.

## Cancellation

Customers can cancel their subscription from the Billing page in their
account settings.

Cancellation prevents future renewal charges. The account remains active
until the end of the current paid billing period.

## Refund processing time

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 5 to 10
business days. Processing time may depend on the customer's bank or card
provider.
